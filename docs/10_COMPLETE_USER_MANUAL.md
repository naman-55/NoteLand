# NoteLand — Complete User Manual

Welcome to **NoteLand**, your private, distraction-free, offline-first personal knowledge sanctuary on Android.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### The First Launch
When you first open NoteLand, you will be greeted by the **Dashboard**. From here, you can monitor your knowledge stats, jump straight into recent notes, or launch creative tools like the **Sticky Board** and **Infinite Canvas**.

### Navigation Structure
NoteLand features an intuitive navigation bar at the bottom:
- 🏠 **Dashboard**: High-level overview, quick stats, and launchpad.
- 📝 **Notes**: Main knowledge repository with folders, tags, and search.
- 📌 **Sticky Board**: Spatial corkboard with free-form movable cards.
- 🎨 **Canvas**: Infinite whiteboard for drawing, sketches, and flowcharts.
- ⚙️ **Settings**: Themes, security vault setup, backup/restore, and diagnostics.

---

## 2. Managing Notes

### Creating a Note
1. Tap the **`+` (New Note)** Floating Action Button on the Dashboard or Notes screen.
2. Enter a descriptive title in the top header.
3. Start typing in the rich text editing area.
4. Changes are saved automatically in real-time.

### Rich Text Formatting Bar
Docked directly above your keyboard is NoteLand's formatting bar:
- **H1 / H2 / H3**: Format section headings.
- **Bold (`**text**`)**: Emphasize important concepts.
- **Italic (`*text*`)**: Subtle emphasis.
- **Strikethrough (`~~text~~`)**: Mark completed or retracted concepts.
- **Inline Code (` `code` `) & Code Block**: Format snippets.
- **Checklist (`- [ ]`)**: Create interactive to-do lists. Tapping a checkbox automatically toggles between `[ ]` and `[x]`.
- **Bullet & Numbered Lists**: Organize sequential ideas.

### Categorizing with Folders & Tags
- **Folders**: Organize notes into hierarchical structures (e.g., Work > Projects > Q4).
- **Tags**: Add cross-cutting labels (e.g., `#urgent`, `#ideas`, `#review`) to group related notes regardless of folder location.

---

## 3. Spatial Corkboard: Sticky Notes

The Sticky Board offers a tactile, visual way to organize short thoughts, brainstorm ideas, or track ongoing tasks.

1. Switch to the **Sticky Board** tab.
2. Tap **Add Sticky** to create a new card.
3. **Move Cards**: Drag and drop any card across the screen. Its exact 2D coordinate is saved instantly.
4. **Accessible Nudge Controls**: If drag gestures are difficult, select a sticky note and use the directional arrow buttons in the bottom toolbar to nudge cards with precision.
5. **Color Coding**: Tap the palette icon on any card to assign vibrant pastel shades.

## 4. Infinite Vector Canvas

The Canvas turns your device into an expansive whiteboard for sketches, diagrams, flowcharts, and mind-maps.

- **Pen Tool**: Pressure-responsive vector drawing.
- **Highlighter**: Semi-transparent strokes to emphasize elements.
- **Shapes**: Draw perfect rectangles and circles with a single gesture.
- **Connectors & Arrows**: Link concepts and create workflow diagrams.
- **Eraser**: Tap or scrub to remove elements.
- **Undo / Redo**: Step back or forward through your creative actions.

## 5. Media, PDF Hub & Document Editor Suite

### PDF Hub & Office Tools Suite
Accessible directly from the Navigation Drawer or Quick Create menu, NoteLand includes a full-featured offline PDF and office tool suite:
- **Recents Carousel**: Visual preview cards of recently marked-up documents (e.g., *Quick Start Guide.pdf*, *What's New.pdf*) with 1-tap clear.
- **Office Tools**:
  - 📷 **Scanner & OCR**: Convert physical camera snaps and gallery photos into searchable, high-resolution multi-page PDF documents.
  - 🔄 **Convert PDF**: Instantly export rich markdown notes and checklists into formatted, publication-ready vector PDFs.
  - ✍️ **Assign Signers & Stamps**: Apply official vector stamps (*APPROVED*, *CONFIDENTIAL*, *REVIEWED*) or your personal digital signature.
  - 📤 **File Sharing**: Share annotated documents securely via the standard Android share sheet.
- **Document Tools**:
  - 📄 **Create PDFs**: Generate blank or ruled digital paper for contracts, minutes, and journals.
  - ✨ **Smart AI Text Tools**: Offline NLP text extractor, word metrics, and automatic extractive summarization.
  - 🗜️ **Compress PDF**: Optimize attachment storage and eliminate unnecessary overhead.

### Full PDF Reader & Annotation Tools
When viewing any PDF document, NoteLand launches a dedicated reader optimized for handheld phones, foldables, and tablets:
- **Top Bar Controls**:
  - ℹ️ **Reading Stats**: View live page counts, estimated words, reading duration (~220 wpm), and total active annotations.
  - 🔊 **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Read Aloud**: Native on-device speech engine that reads document text aloud with play/pause and speed control (0.75x–1.5x).
  - 🔍 **In-Document Search**: Instant offline keyword search with match counter and match jumping.
  - 👁️ **Reading Themes**: Switch between **Daylight White**, **Night Charcoal**, **Warm Sepia** (paper tint), and **Mint Green** (eye protection).
  - 📤 **Share**: Export the document with all embedded annotations.
- **Vertical Floating Annotation Toolbar (Right-Hand Side)**:
  - 📝 **Sticky Notes**: Tap anywhere on a page to anchor a yellow comment badge (`≡`). Tapping the badge opens a popup bubble to view or edit comments.
  - 🖊️ **Text Highlighter**: Highlight text passages in vibrant yellow, sky blue, mint green, coral pink, or orange.
  - <s>S</s> **Strikethrough**: Draw precise strike lines through retracted clauses or sentences.
  - <u>U</u> **Underline**: Straight or wavy underlines for important references.
  - ✏️ **Handwrite / Pen**: Smooth stylus and finger handwriting with customizable brush width (2–14dp), opacity, and color palette.
  - 🔲 **Shapes**: Draw vector rectangles, circles/ovals (e.g., highlighting headings), direction arrows with heads, and 5-pointed stars (⭐).
  - 🔤 **Text Box**: Add custom typed text directly onto any page with custom styling.
  - 🏷️ **Stamps**: Place vector stamps (*APPROVED*, *CONFIDENTIAL*, *REVIEWED*) or custom signatures.
  - 🧹 **Eraser**: Scrub or tap over any annotation to delete it.
  - ▦ **Thumbnails**: Open a visual grid of all document pages for 1-tap jumping.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar**:
  - `<` / `>` Previous & Next page navigation.
  - 📐 **View Mode**: Fit-to-screen and 100% zoom reset.
  - 📄 **Page Jump Pill**: Jump to any specific page number via numeric input or slider.
  - 🔖 **Bookmark Ribbon**: Bookmark important pages with separate SQLite indexing.

## 6. Offline Smart Utilities

Tap the **Smart Tools (⚡)** button in any note to access local text utilities:
- **Metrics**: Instant word count, character count, and estimated reading time.
- **Extractive Summary**: Automatically generates a 3-sentence summary of long notes.
- **Checklist Extractor**: Finds action items in your text and converts them into structured checkboxes.
- **Suggested Title**: Analyzes note content to propose concise, clear titles.
- **Duplicate Detector**: Alerts you if similar thoughts already exist elsewhere in your knowledge base.

## 7. Security & Privacy Vault

### Setting Up Your Master PIN
1. Go to **Settings** > **Vault Security**.
2. Tap **Set Master PIN** and enter a 4–8 digit PIN.
3. (Optional) Enable **Biometric Unlock** for fingerprint or face authentication.

### Locking Sensitive Notes
1. Open any note you wish to protect.
2. Tap the **Overflow Menu (⋮)** > **Lock Note**.
3. The note is immediately encrypted using AES-256-GCM. It will no longer show plaintext previews or appear in normal search results.

### Hidden Notes
If you simply want to declutter your notes list without encrypting content, select **Hide Note**. Hidden notes are moved to the unlisted Hidden Vault.

## 8. Offline Backup & Data Export

Your data is yours forever. NoteLand includes a complete export/restore engine:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Backup & Restore**.
2. Tap **Export Backup (ZIP)**. NoteLand packages all notes, folders, tags, canvas elements, sticky notes, and attachments into a compressed archive.
3. Save the ZIP file to your preferred local folder or external drive.
4. To restore: Tap **Restore from Backup**, select a valid NoteLand ZIP file, inspect the contents preview, and choose **Merge** (safe append) or **Replace** (full restore).
