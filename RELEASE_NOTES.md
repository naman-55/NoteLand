# NoteLand 3.5 — Release Notes

NoteLand 3.5 brings a fully unified creative, productivity, and organization ecosystem to your device.

## ✨ New Features

### 🎨 NoteLand Studio 
- **Unified Workspace:** Mind Maps, Whiteboards (Canvas), and Sticky Walls all live together in a sleek new interface.
- **Multiple Sticky Walls:** You can now create and swap between different named Sticky Walls.
- **Convert Notes to Mind Maps:** Instantly generate a visual Mind Map from the structure of any text note.
- **Export Everywhere:** You can now export Canvas and Mind Maps not just as PNGs, but as high-quality PDFs and JPGs.
- **Send to Sticky Wall:** Highlight text in a standard Note and instantly beam it as a card onto your active Sticky Wall.

### 🎯 Productivity Suite 
- **Segmented Planner:** Toggle your schedule view between Daily, Weekly, and Monthly overviews.
- **Multiple Kanban Boards:** Manage distinct projects with isolated columns and task pipelines.
- **Goals with Milestones:** Build complex Goals, define strict Deadlines, and split them out into actionable milestones (Pending vs. Completed).
- **Global Search Filters:** Filter your active tasks by Priority, or search for titles instantly.

### ⚙️ Integrations & Settings
- **System-Wide Open & Share:** NoteLand now registers natively with Android. Select a PDF, Image, or text file from WhatsApp or your File Manager, tap "Share", and NoteLand will smoothly intercept it. You can open files temporarily or save them straight to a folder.
- **Streamlined Customization:** Manage your App Theme, Quick Edit Toolbar, Storage limits, and Vault PIN easily from the revamped Settings Hub.

## 🐛 Fixes & Improvements
- Corrected Note card colors and list margins on the Home screen.
- Resolved navigation crash issues when swapping Kanban filters.
- Optimized performance for heavy image and background worker states.
